Animated locations: four 2D lane-defense maps with extended ambient loops.
Resolution: backgrounds and transparent overlays 1672 x 941 pixels.
Each scene has 24 frames named overlay_000.png through overlay_023.png.
Playback: 12 frames per second, loop after 2 seconds. Composite overlays over the matching background with normal alpha blending.
GIF previews combine the background and effects at 960 x 540 for quick viewing.
forest-outpost: distant birds, waving flags and tent fabric, flickering lanterns, drifting leaves.
ruined-cemetery: hands emerging among foreground graves, blinking crypt doorway, moving mist and candles.
marsh-crossing: hopping frogs, falling droplets, water ripples and fireflies.
frost-pass: denser snow, wind streaks, moving flames and waving banners.
The full-resolution static backgrounds and build_animations.py are included.
Rebuilding requires Python and Pillow.
