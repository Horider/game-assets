# Generation prompts

All nine source strips were made with the built-in image generator and `transparent_background: true`. The shared constraints were detailed hand-painted medieval fantasy pixel art, true alpha transparency, isolated subjects, no text, no borders, and equal cells in a single horizontal row.

1. **Axe impact dust:** Five sequential frames: tiny dirt burst, wide radial tan dust burst, billowing cloud and flying grit, thinning cloud, sparse fading motes. Consistent ground anchor; no axe or character.
2. **Original archer arrows:** Four isolated right-pointing arrows at the same scale: plain wood and iron with gray fletching; reinforced steel broadhead with blue fletching; engraved silver with teal fletching; magical gold and silver with cyan runes and glowing blue feathers. They now represent levels 3, 5, 7 and 10. No bow or character.
3. **Healing trace:** Five sequential green and gold healing effects: first glints, forming circular sigil, bright ring with an illuminated cross and rising particles, softening ring with a spiral, fading motes. No caster.
4. **Sword swing trace:** Five sequential silver-white slash effects: short streak, growing arc, bright crescent, broken fading arc, sparse motes. No sword or character.
5. **Sword swing:** Five poses of the same steel longsword with a dark leather grip and modest gold crossguard: raised, diagonal, horizontal, low follow-through, settling. No hand or slash trail. For design consistency, the exported poses were built by rotating one generated sword from this source strip.
6. **Paladin shield, level 1:** Five guard, tilt, impact, rebound, guard poses of the same pointed dark-blue wooden shield with iron rim and silver fleur-de-lis.
7. **Paladin shield, level 2:** Five corresponding poses of a reinforced dark-blue steel shield with polished silver rim, rivets, and larger silver fleur-de-lis.
8. **Paladin shield, level 3:** Five corresponding poses of engraved polished steel with navy enamel, silver fleur-de-lis, and gold trim.
9. **Paladin shield, level 4:** Five corresponding poses of an ornate silver and gold shield with royal-blue enamel, glowing pale-gold fleur-de-lis, and restrained sacred light.

## Additional arrow levels

Each of the six added arrows was generated as one right-pointing transparent projectile in the same medieval fantasy pixel-art style and scale as the original arrows, with the neighboring original level PNGs supplied as visual references.

- **Level 1:** Rough wood, short worn gray-white feathers, tiny dull iron point tied with cord; simpler than original level 3.
- **Level 2:** Polished wood, clean gray-white feathers, compact sharpened iron leaf point and one plain collar; still below level 3.
- **Level 4:** Dark wood, slate-gray feathers with restrained blue tips, sharper steel broadhead and small blue collar; visually between levels 3 and 5, without magical glow.
- **Level 6:** Blue-to-teal feathers, engraved silver-steel head and small cyan setting; visually between levels 5 and 7, with only a faint hint of magic.
- **Level 8:** Layered teal-blue feathers, etched silver head, small gold inlays and cyan gem with restrained aura; between levels 7 and 10.
- **Level 9:** Bright blue-cyan feathers, tiny cyan shaft runes and silver-gold crystal head with a stronger controlled glow; just below level 10.
